RAILWORKS Alpha 0.5 — New Frontier

World 180 × 140 grid nodes, no prebuilt rails, trains or industry. Manually place mines in resource deposits and factories on clear land, stations beside track. Rail planner supports endpoint selection, preview and curved turnouts.

The rail graph is still eight-direction (45-degree increments) and does not yet reproduce Factorio 2.0's full 22.5-degree library.
