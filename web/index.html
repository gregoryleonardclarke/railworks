Railworks · Railway Logistics

Design and operate a railway network. Factories produce automatically; your job is to keep the freight moving without creating bottlenecks.

Build the network

  • R: Click your start point or existing rail node. Move the cursor to preview a rail alignment that accounts for approach direction. Click to place it, and click further destinations to extend. Branch off existing rails to build Y and T junctions.
  • Rail geometry: Straight runs, 45° transitioning bends, genuine curved turnout transitions, and diamond crossings. Train wheels follow the exact paths being rendered rather than cutting corners. Sharp 90° turns are not accepted by the route finder.
  • Ctrl+Z: Undo only the newly built segments from your most recent construction. Use Esc to finish drawing.
  • J: Click a point with three or more connections to cycle its manual preferred exit. Trains route through points automatically, unless you enable “Obey manual switch points” on the selected locomotive.
  • B: Click a rail to install a block signal. Only permits entry when its guarded block is unoccupied.
  • C: Click a rail to install a chain signal. Tries to reserve the full path through a junction to a clear exit.
  • F: Reverse the facing direction of the signal you are placing. Each click cycles placement and removal. H: Colour the occupancy blocks.

Keep industry supplied

  • West Timber Mill makes timber automatically.
  • Ironworks makes steel automatically.
  • Central Depot consumes two timber + one steel to manufacture machinery.
  • North Distribution buys machinery. Deliveries earn money.
  • Choose a train's freight order, then send it to a source and to its consumer. Wagons load/unload at scheduled stops.
  • S: Add station, T: add locomotive, X: demolish track or signal.

Current limitations: track-construction points still snap to an eight-direction grid, curve radius and 22.5° rail variants are simplified rather than matching Factorio's complete 2.0 rail planner; single-track occupancy; full two-way paired signalling, sophisticated deadlock recovery, configurable factory recipes and rail construction costs remain in development. Save is manual; use Export/Import to transfer a railway between browser installations or itch.io uploads.
