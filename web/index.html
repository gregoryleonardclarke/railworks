Railworks · Railway Logistics

Design and operate a railway network. Start with no railway or buildings. Place mines at resources, factories on clear land and stations on rails near your buildings.

Build the network

  • R: Click open ground for the first rail or an existing rail connection to extend. Preview the green ghost alignment, then click to build and continue. Press R again to rotate the starting piece; Escape or right-click cancels.
  • Rail geometry: Continuous extruded steel rails and ballast form smoothly rounded 45° bends and curved turnout transitions. Sleepers follow true arc length. Train wheels follow the rendered curves; sharp 90° turns are not accepted.
  • Camera: Hold right mouse and drag horizontally to orbit freely, vertically to change the viewing angle. Middle-drag or Shift+right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom, hold Q/E for continuous rotation, or use the rotation buttons in the sidebar.
  • Ctrl+Z: Undo only the newly built segments from your most recent construction. Use Esc to finish drawing.
  • J: Click a point with three or more connections to cycle its manual preferred exit. Trains route through points automatically, unless you enable “Obey manual switch points” on the selected locomotive.
  • B: Click a rail to install a block signal. Only permits entry when its guarded block is unoccupied.
  • C: Click a rail to install a chain signal. Tries to reserve the full path through a junction to a clear exit.
  • F: Reverse the facing direction of the signal you are placing. Each click cycles placement and removal. H: Colour the occupancy blocks.

Keep industry supplied

  • Logging camp: build inside the green forest zone to produce timber.
  • Ore mine: build inside grey-blue ore deposits to produce steel.
  • Machine works: build on clear ground; needs two timber and one steel delivered by rail for machinery.
  • Distribution hub: build anywhere on clear ground to receive machinery deliveries.
  • Choose a train's freight order, then send it to a source and to its consumer. Wagons load/unload at scheduled stops.
  • 1–4: Place manual industry sites. S: Place a station on rail near a facility. T: Place a locomotive. X: Demolish.

Current limitations: this is a Factorio-inspired eight-direction rail planner, NOT yet a complete implementation of its 22.5° pieces, obstruction avoidance and interlocking; full two-way paired signalling, sophisticated deadlock recovery, configurable factory recipes and rail construction costs remain in development. Save is manual; use Export/Import to transfer a railway between browser installations or itch.io uploads.
